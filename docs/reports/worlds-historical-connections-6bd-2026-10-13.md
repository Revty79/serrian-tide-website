Authors can create independently dated historical connection accounts for an existing map/place, choose an available destination and optional arrival, or explicitly record unavailable, obsolete or unknown coverage. Periods are inclusive and cannot overlap for the same map/place/timeline. Shared connection identities and preferences remain unchanged. Each new account captures the exact shared connection identity/revision/endpoints when present. Edits, archive and restore append immutable versions. Alternate timelines retain an exact direct-parent source deliberately; interpretations and later parent edits remain independent.

Explorer preserves the World, timeline and year across linked maps, reload and browser navigation. A historical destination overrides baseline preferences and associated-map alternatives. Explicit unknown/unavailable/obsolete choices suppress inferred destinations. A gap retains shared navigation only as a labeled reference with unknown historical connection coverage. Destinations without an appearance at that year show their shared baseline with unknown coverage. Informational findings identify missing branch coverage, archived/unavailable arrivals, not-established places and differing local geographic or political accounts. They never change maps, claims or History events.

Read-only comparison accepts two deliberate selections of the same map: years, selected-timeline appearances or exact retained revisions. It reuses the original cartography, settlement and political renderers, with exact political/History source readers. Saved differences cover physical geometry, local place accounts, settlement layouts/conditions, territories/relationships, independent historical connection choices, source accounts and provenance. Unknown coverage is distinguished from saved differences. Comparison does not reconcile sources or discard an open map draft.

Both ordinary URL aliases use server filtering before serialization. Protected connection accounts, protected destination/arrival subjects, private revisions and unadopted parent sources are withheld. Current protection and exact retained source protection both apply. Navigation cannot bypass a withheld historical choice through an associated map. Ordinary viewing and explicit Administrator review remain read-only; preview-qualified mutations are rejected. Foreign users and Players are denied. Context guards remove old appearance/comparison information during loading, failed requests and retry. Public source accounts are also withheld in ordinary projections.

Failed saves retain connection drafts. Acknowledged writes followed by failed readback recover through the acknowledged identity and block another write until readback. Client request IDs also make retried creation idempotent. Two-tab conflicts expose an explicit draft/saved-source comparison; the author deliberately keeps the draft against the compared revision or accepts the saved account. Dirty dismissal and browser navigation are guarded; drafts can be downloaded.

New evidence is retained under `artifacts/guidance/worlds-historical-connections/`; earlier evidence directories and the completed 6B-C database were not changed. Tests used a new database cloned from completed 6B-C evidence, new disposable Worlds and an isolated production build/server. All original rows across the prior 102 World tables were compared and preserved, including immutable sources, earlier timeline heads, stable identities, Realm links, Calendar/Chronology/History pins and editable artwork.
