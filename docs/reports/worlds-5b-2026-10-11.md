## 4. Recurrence and precision

BigInt rational arithmetic preserves integers, decimals and fractions without float drift; comparisons, negative floor/modulo, offsets and event windows are exact. Authored inputs support 24 integer digits and twelve decimal places or denominator digits, reject scientific notation/zero denominators, and keep the raw source string. Computed positions support longer internal fractions without weakening input validation. Signed canonical years retain the trillion-year limit; shared elapsed days retain the existing twenty-digit whole-position grammar. Hours/minutes require an anchored version with explicit day units. Known phase durations must sum exactly to the period; unknown periods, anchors or qualitative duration sequences explain their calculation limits. Applicable beginnings/endings stop projections. No intervening years or days are iterated.
