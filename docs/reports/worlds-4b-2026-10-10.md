Individuals now have a dedicated Worlds workspace. A name is sufficient. Optional grouped fields describe identity, physical or nonphysical existence, personality, knowledge, social affiliations and biography. Original beings, collective consciousnesses and immortals require no conventional biological life stages. Creator-defined sections remain available. Saved historical names/titles retain dates, meanings and perspectives against one underlying identity. Search includes names, aliases, titles and visible historical name accounts; creator classification, lifecycle and saved-place filters organize the library.

`world_lore_identity.family = individual` extends the existing stable registry. `world_individual_version` holds typed immutable detail; `world_individual_name` and `world_relationship_period` hold version-bound personal accounts. Relationships use existing independently saved relationship identities and participant rows, with original roles and any supported number of progenitors/creators/companions. Periods preserve independent optional beginnings/endings, ongoing/ended/unknown/disputed status and perspective. No ancestry account becomes objective truth. Source versions, heads, audit, request retries, owner locks and revision checks retain their established contracts.

Life milestones save directly into canonical World History. Corrections preserve the event ID and append a source; biography-only edits create no event. One event can link several individuals and places. Readers navigate from the individual to History and back to Individuals, or to existing Peoples and Atlas records. Geographic associations retain stable Atlas identities and immutable source dates/protection; no map, route or mechanical Character is duplicated or moved.

Individual biographies require explicit whole-source review in alternate timelines. Relationship periods and lore sources naming individual participants also require review, because an early header date cannot prove every biographical claim predates divergence. Canonical events remain independent: Aralyn's birth at 810 and achievement at 830 persist before the start-of-835 divergence; Primary death at 840 stays outside accepted alternate History; survival at 840 and appointment as queen at 845 are authored only in the child. Parent acceptance/adoption is revision checked, local interpretations retain the same identity, and nested pins remain independent. This is conservative narrative source authoring, without a complete living-population simulation.

Privacy is enforced before serialization. Protected fields, names, periods, participants, sections, author notes, geography and events are omitted from ordinary knowledge. Private reputation starts protected by default. Pending/excluded identities and unadopted parent sources are omitted too. A relationship with a protected participant is withheld from ordinary metadata; inverse discovery filters hidden sources before its limit. Milestones authored through a relationship with protected participants/periods are conservatively protected. Hidden metadata does not enter category/name search. World ownership and explicit read-only Administrator review remain mandatory; Campaign membership grants no access.

The editor progressively discloses optional groups. It retains unsaved and failed drafts, rejects unchanged stale submissions, and offers explicit latest-record recovery. Actual desktop and native-touch phone sessions exercise authoring, corrections, relationship periods, geographic selectors, History navigation, alternate source adoption, search/pagination, archive/restore and read-only review. Existing semantic appearance and field guidance are reused.

All required local checks are complete; the final full production regression passed. The temporary databases are removed afterward; the fixture and retained receipt reproduce the scenario without inserting demonstration records into DEV or Production.
