## Discovery and privacy authority

[world-atlas-discovery-service.ts](../../src/features/worlds/world-atlas-discovery-service.ts) authorizes the World and selected timeline before returning labels, records or maps. It reuses existing place milestones, lore-geography, Realm-map links, society-time links, Town/Shop bindings and saved Campaign contexts. Physical political relationships use [native-map-history-territory-discovery.ts](../../src/features/worlds/native-map-history-territory-discovery.ts). Floors and underground levels are offered only through their actual saved Place bindings and a resolvable dated component. Craft has its actual saved World placement and native entrances; it acquires no fabricated Town or lore relationship.

Historical positions come from accepted dated components. Unknown dates never borrow a current marker, building, floor or dungeon. Recorded association periods are checked independently of map coverage. Exact lore/history/version hints in navigation are reauthorized server-side. Ordinary discovery returns no native positions, hidden identities, private map counts or Realm-map choices. Administrator review remains read-only; private image review authorizes the retained version and rejects an unused replacement. Campaign membership is never a substitute for World access.

[world-atlas-discovery-client.tsx](../../src/features/worlds/world-atlas-discovery-client.tsx) provides the Find in Atlas choices and contextual inspector. Cross-record browsing opens separately and supplies a constrained same-World return. [atlas-record-view.ts](../../src/features/worlds/atlas-record-view.ts) binds saved camera coordinates to the actual map/context. Unsaved record and map work stays mounted; ordinary/review flags and dates travel with the destination. Internal current-floor synchronization preserves existing editor guards; a real date/source change still uses historical Pending Work protection.

## Appropriate limits

Calendars, dating systems, eras, abstract Realms, general lore and Campaign contexts are not intrinsically physical locations. Supported real relationships provide contextual navigation. Otherwise the UI states that no geographic relationship or map representation is established. The pass does not infer locations from names, copy modern relationships into a historical year, add positions for unsupported abstract concepts, implement Campaign movement, or create a second relationship registry.

Political native geometry declares physical metres and an exact accepted source/extent. Legacy `atlas-v1` geometry remains in its original coordinate space and validator. No legacy political boundary is silently projected onto numerical terrain. Regions show the actual dated Root authority and open that Root for deliberate political editing.

Browser receipts are technical installed-Chrome evidence using task-owned services, including native phone touch emulation. They do not constitute owner artistic/usability acceptance or physical-device certification. The delivery report records exact final sources, failed attempts, migrations, performance and publication status.
