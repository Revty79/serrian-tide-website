## Remaining detail and interpretation limits

- Small islands, narrow fjords, delta distributaries, minor straits and intricate coasts outside selected finer sources remain generalized or omitted. A 39 km base sample cannot represent them faithfully by adding more outline vertices alone.
- Broad height bands, inherited coarse slopes and some abrupt support transitions remain visible, especially in close regional views and strongly shaded styles. These are disclosed in the retained exports. The saved terrain remains editable; photorealistic terrain and survey-grade vertical accuracy are not delivered.
- River coverage comprises selected main-stem and connecting reaches. It does not cover all headwaters, tributaries, reservoirs, distributaries or the full St Lawrence Gulf. An open endpoint marks an unfinished cartographic reach. A lake with no authored outlet has a closed native record; that is **not a claim that the real lake is endorheic**. Only explicitly checked connections are certified here.
- River widths remain symbolic rather than measured bank widths. No discharge, catchment area, seasonal flooding, flow simulation or hydraulic certification is claimed. Lake levels and basin floors are descriptive cartography.
- Polar cover has mixed observation dates. Smaller Greenland nunataks, Antarctic outcrops, non-polar glacier coverage and complete land-cover transitions remain incomplete. Arid-region coverage does not mean uniform vegetation or a current ecological survey.
- Thirty-six visible sample inspections compare representative native heights with independently averaged reference cells. They expose substantial local differences, often hundreds of metres and occasionally more, rather than demonstrating DEM equivalence. Coastal mixed cells are especially sensitive to shoreline placement and sampling convention. The detailed values remain private.
