# External source licensing policy

Owner requirement effective 2026-11-09. Applies permanently to Project Earth and future Serrian Tide imported assets and geographic data. Serrian Tide is a commercial subscription website/game. Source review precedes incorporation, including tracing, sampling, generalizing, hand-authoring from a reference, extracting labels or deriving terrain. A manual editing step does not erase provenance.

## Intake decision

No paid-license data, purchases, trials, promised future licenses, or research/education-only permissions. Download price, public access, a government domain, the word “open”, a repository's software license and an author's supplied license string do not establish rights over the actual data.

Prefer provider-confirmed public domain or CC0. A commercially permissive attribution license can pass only when its exact obligations can be met in the product. Reject NC, ND, incompatible redistribution terms and commercial restrictions. Hold missing/ambiguous terms, uncertain upstream ownership and unverified editions. ODbL and other share-alike sources require a separate documented review before use: commercial permission alone does not resolve derivative/collective database, public-use, disclosure, customer-access, sublicensing or export obligations. Do not assume these licenses apply to the whole application, or assume they cannot affect the geographic database.

Check the download/service/API agreement independently of the dataset license. Use authorized endpoints, quotas and access methods. Do not scrape restricted map services, reuse commercial map tiles as data, bypass authentication, or evade refusal/rate limits. A freely licensed dataset may need a different lawful delivery channel. A hosting platform's name or an Esri file format does not determine the license of every third-party dataset hosted there.

## Required inventory entry

Record the following before acceptance, with a dated review and an explicit decision:

- Review ID, provider/rightsholder, dataset/product, exact edition, represented dates, retrieval date, official dataset and license URLs, complete upstream lineage and material-specific exceptions.
- Exact source file/checksum or immutable release identifier; retain the terms/evidence and their retrieval time privately. A URL named `latest` alone is insufficient. If a provider does not publish an edition, record that fact and identify the retained bytes.
- Cost and service/API terms, approved access channel, limits and redistribution restrictions. No paid/trial assumption.
- Rights separately assessed for commercial subscription website/game display, modifications/derivatives, customer access, digital map images/documents, printed maps and any offered editable/vector/database downloads. An unoffered output remains forbidden until reviewed.
- Required author/provider notices, copyright, license link/text, source link, modification notices, disclaimer/no-endorsement and downstream restrictions. Distinguish obligations from voluntary credits.
- Exact affected saved records, immutable versions, derived geometry/numerical sources, historical copies and exports; track both direct imports and reference-guided work. Keep private identifiers and actual reference content outside public Git.
- Decision: `source-rights-verified`, `conditional-product-compliance`, `hold-unverified`, `hold-special-review` or `rejected-for-use`, with reasons, missing evidence, replacement plan and reviewer/date. These labels document a review; they are not legal warranties or automatic runtime enforcement.

Source rights and product compliance are separate gates. “Source rights verified” does not approve an entire mixed-source map. A changed edition, access service, upstream source, license, use, export format or customer entitlement requires renewed review. Approval for an image does not imply approval for the underlying vector/database download.

## Product and output gate

Preserve external credits separately from **Built by Revty79** or another creator. Creator credit describes Serrian Tide authorship, not ownership of external material. Required notices must survive saved source records, ordinary map viewing, history/variations/published copies, the exact layers included in an export, PNG/JPEG/SVG/PDF and print/poster pages or their complete accompanying source pages. License links need usable printed URLs where required. Describe modifications without implying provider endorsement.

Required credits cannot be removed by hiding optional creator/context labels, changing style, cropping, switching scale, or excluding optional artwork. Fail an export if necessary credits cannot fit or be delivered completely. Respect the rights recipients receive in licensed material; proprietary product terms and technical controls must not remove rights granted by that license. Do not attach a blanket exclusive copyright claim to public-domain material. CC BY does not automatically require the proprietary application code to become open source; its notices and recipient rights still apply to the covered material.

Before marking compliance passed, demonstrate actual saved/reopened provenance, map-view credits, representative historical/published-copy behavior, every offered output format and printed/poster credit layout. Tests must cover optional-label hiding, crops, revisions, mixed sources and failed/incomplete credit delivery. A document listing credits alone does not pass this gate. Missing native source lineage or export propagation is a blocker to releasing that content.

## Existing material and failures

Stop using an unverified or incompatible source immediately. Preserve accepted versions, original files and truthful provenance. Record affected identities/versions/outputs and descendants, mark an operational hold and prevent the agent from further derivation, release or distribution. Do not silently delete, overwrite or hide origin, erase failures, reset shared data, or rename external derivation as creator-authored. Record clearly whether holds are documentary or actually enforced by the application.

Propose a no-cost compatible replacement. Verify the replacement's own terms and geographic accuracy before normal visible authoring; preserve the old accepted history and identity. Do not claim an independent replacement while reusing the restricted geometry or imagery. Adding a new credit is not a remedy for incompatible permission. Unresolved rights or a major database/share-alike architecture choice goes to owner review. No purchase or external contact is authorized by this policy.

The [Earth inventory](project-earth-licensing-inventory.md) is the initial application of this policy. Its failed commercial gate supersedes older technical-completion wording for release readiness. Production, Canon and deployment remain separately unauthorized.
