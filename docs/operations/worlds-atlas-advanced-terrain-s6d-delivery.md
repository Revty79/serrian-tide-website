## Numerical operations

**River carving.** Native river drawing/acceptance saves water records without modifying heights. Carving deliberately selects a saved river and control-point segment, or its eligible connected downstream course. Controls use metres for width/depth, percentage strength, bank softness and bed smoothing. The engine resolves saved graph identities, checks the current sampled route for uphill crossings, stops at supported outlet/sink/lake endpoints, and lowers only a bounded smooth corridor. Quartic lateral weights soften the banks. Maximum per-sample cutting is capped by chosen depth × strength, including steep neighboring banks; narrow streams cannot implicitly become canyons. Unsupported sub-sample widths require finer terrain or an explicitly larger width. Sea, lake/wetland supports and settlement supports remain protected. A materially changed/uphill route requires water review rather than forced excavation.

**Erosion.** Directional runoff and gravity/talus modes move material toward an eligible lower neighbor. Runoff direction is clockwise from north with an adjustable directional influence; talus transport uses the chosen repose angle. Strength, duration, maximum wear, deposition and edge feathering are explicit. Each iteration accumulates simultaneous transport before updating the bed. This is a localized stylized mapmaking model, distinct from a smooth/flatten average. With deposition enabled, transfers balance within eligible samples before metre rounding, sea/elevation clamps and wear bounds; exact physical material conservation is not claimed. With deposition off, material leaves the model. Reported cut/fill volumes expose the accepted quantized result.

**Volcano/caldera.** Cone, broad shield, composite, cratered mountain and caldera forms share numerical radial profile logic. Creators choose the centre, radius, height above existing land, slope profile, crater diameter/depth, transition width, strength and bounded east/south summit offset. The landform adds to existing relief, with crater depression and an outer blend. Dimensions are constrained against the World, native resolution and influence/work budgets. Subsequent ordinary sculpting edits the same terrain. Immutable native source metadata retains the authored volcano intent even after those later edits; selecting a historical intent does not assert that its current shape is unchanged.

**Eruptions.** Numerical summit collapse lowers a bounded land area. Numerical lava follows accepted pre-eruption downhill gradients from an explicitly chosen volcano rim/source, adds a bounded deposit, and stops at the chosen extent, step limit, sink, shore or protected boundary. It is a 2D authoring approximation, without real-time fluid dynamics. Ash/debris is explicitly **visual only**: native protected bare environmental cover beneath existing manual paint. Its preview/acceptance changes no height samples or terrain revision. Acceptance creates an ordinary undoable Environment draft; **Save environment** remains explicit. Existing artwork/private asset controls can decorate the scene independently.

Canonical event dates are deferred: existing narrative history entries do not provide an accepted numerical-operation event/date binding. Native revision author/time and versioned operation intent remain retained for future historical-state integration. No era-specific terrain copy, historical playback, collaboration or animation is added.
