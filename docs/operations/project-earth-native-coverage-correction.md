Both axes retain their previous size or grow; at least one must grow. Offsets use complete 32-sample tiles. Supported dimensions, the 4096-axis advanced confirmation, resource budget, fixed World edges, parent containment and sibling exclusion retain their existing limits. Children keep their original parent revision pins. Their coverage remains protected. The new outer collar remains inherited; a former edge inside the enlarged area becomes eligible for ordinary sculpting.

Cancellation changes no accepted terrain. The first acceptance packet and its recovered retry use the same normalized bytes. An interrupted response can be retried after normal reload without adding another source. Older saved revisions retain their original geometry. Restoring one replaces its retained area while keeping coverage added afterward; Undo/Redo of subsequent edits continues through the existing save paths.
