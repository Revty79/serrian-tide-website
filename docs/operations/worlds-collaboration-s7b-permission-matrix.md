## Canon supplement

An Administrator may explicitly publish or withdraw **whole-World canon** through a separate confirmed, revisioned, audited action. Canon originals reject content writes from every role, including their owner, and marking retires the previous editing fence. Administrators retain full read-only review of all protected and ordinary World content; this visibility does not become content editing.

Signed-in Players may read ordinary canon content in all reviewed families except Campaign associations. Protected source graphs, notes, fields, hidden objects, original private libraries and unrelated catalog identities keep their existing restrictions. While canon is published, every non-owner participant uses the same ordinary read-only view, including accounts with older narrow/protected invitations. Protected material remains in Owner/Administrator review; the original invitation settings are retained and resume after canon withdrawal. No narrow protected grant is broadened across public sections. Anonymous users receive no canon access.

Every eligible Player may use the dedicated canon-pattern route to create a private independent World, with copied opening prose and safe active Primary History under new identities. They may create their own timelines, maps and stories there using normal session/tab fences. Canon maps and lore remain read-only reference destinations; their geometry, assets and linked identities are not cloned. This narrow creation path grants no global role or arbitrary blank-World creation. The personal version remains after a source's canon withdrawal; new source reads and variations obey current access/decision revisions. Exact requests are retained in account-bound storage with export and explicit discard.

Existing catalog canon roots are read-only through the central authoring guard; authorized Race/Creature/Item pattern copies use a separate permission and remain non-canon under their actual creator.

## Actual families and navigation

The Peoples endpoint independently checks all 33 native families: species, people, origin, relationship, culture, civilization, language, population, belief, tradition, individual, polity, government, institution, office, appointment, claim, agreement, conflict, organization, organizationRole, membership, assignment, network, cosmicFramework, realm, celestial, cosmicPrinciple, cosmicConnection, skyCycle, skyObservation, skyCalendarLink and story. A shared endpoint does not confer all families.

World and Regional maps include current and immutable terrain bytes, refinements, materials, hydrology, environment, appearance, artwork, labels, styles, geographic generation/authoring, hierarchy and revisions. Town, floor, Underground, exterior craft and deck histories retain independent native states and original relationship identities. Discovery, Find in Atlas, related records, labels, source selectors, geographic associations, Realm links, timeline/year navigation, comparisons, private thumbnails and downloads apply the same scope and protected-source checks. A link does not grant its destination.

The private retained-source capture mode is a server-only, read-only closure. It first authorizes the actual actor and required map/history scopes, preserves unshared source components verbatim without impersonating the owner, and cannot execute a write. Its expanded snapshot authority is not an HTTP capability and does not escape into the caller's content projection.
