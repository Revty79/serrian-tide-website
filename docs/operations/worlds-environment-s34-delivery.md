## Creator experience

Open an accepted terrain map in Worlds -> Atlas, expand the map and choose **Climate & Biomes**. Choose a starting preset, adjust broad temperature/moisture/wind assumptions, preview on the accepted terrain, compare the temporary proposal and accept it. **Save environment** explicitly commits it independently. Choosing a preset, moving the camera or finishing a calculation does not accept or save it. Existing maps retain their old artwork until environmental interpretation is deliberately authored/accepted.

Seven presets cover mixed Earth-like bands, predominantly temperate, humid tropical, arid/desert, cold/polar, strongly seasonal and exotic conditions. Advanced assumptions remain collapsed. Configurable thermal bands, uniform reference, north/south gradient and periodic exotic variation avoid silently assigning Earth circumference, latitude, orbit or seasons to a fictional World. The wind selector explicitly describes the direction moisture travels toward.

The same inspector paints biomes or independent vegetation, draws named polygon boundaries, selects existing regions and moves/inserts/removes their points. Creators change classifications, density, illustrative crown scale, clustering and protection. Bare coverage deliberately removes vegetation; a layer-specific eraser returns authored coverage to its accepted automatic interpretation. Removing a region retains all history and restores underlying interpretation. New coverage is protected by default. Undo/redo and completed-edit browser recovery remain independent of terrain/material/water history.

Whole-World review establishes broad context. Selected authored boundaries, accepted numerical refinements or the visible physical frame can receive a local environmental review. Local downscaling retains the accepted global settings and context rather than treating a tiny viewport as an isolated climate. Global regeneration retains all manual coverage by default. An explicit replacement checkbox can remove unprotected coverage wholly contained in the reviewed area; protected identities/geometry always remain. Changing global assumptions deliberately supersedes local automatic fields, without deleting their immutable history or manual coverage.

Visibility separates the environment, biome distribution, vegetation, detailed artwork, modeled temperature and relative moisture. Inspection explains accepted classifications, cover and source spacing. A manually painted region without climate does not display fabricated temperature or moisture. Changes in numerical sources or water retain authoring and show a review warning; they do not automatically regenerate geography. Changed global context must be reviewed before new local downscaling.

Independent saves provide exact original-request retry, optimistic conflict rejection, export, interrupted-save recovery and immutable historical restoration. A lost response after server acceptance can be recovered and retried without another revision. A competing save retains the original draft for export/review. Terrain autosaving/undo and water editing retain pending environmental coverage and its navigation guard. ## Climate and geographic interpretation

`climate-v1` is a deterministic calibrated tendency model, not an atmospheric simulation. It uses an explicit seed/version, configurable thermal assumptions, accepted physical height, lapse cooling, major-water distance, maritime influence, continental dryness, bounded upwind barriers, windward enhancement and provisional seasonal amplitude. Reversing wind exchanges wet/dry tendencies on a deliberate ridge fixture. Tests cover altitude cooling, coast/interior, islands, confirmed wetlands/lakes, exotic settings, wrapped seams, adjacent/nested refinements and million-km Worlds.

Global analysis uses up to 256 by 256 cells from the accepted base. Local analysis uses up to 128 by 128 cells, accepted global context and bounded numerical refinement loading. Each accepted cell packs temperature, relative moisture, biome, vegetation, density and seasonal contrast; exact source revisions, physical frame, analysis spacing, recipe and global-context identity are retained. The same inputs repeat the same recommendations. Camera, style and artwork LOD never alter accepted climate identity. Coarse fields cannot certify detailed environmental boundaries.

Temperature is a simplified mean estimate in C, not measured weather. Moisture is dimensionless; no physically accurate rainfall, river volume, groundwater, flooding or permanence is asserted. The upwind model samples 16 locations within an explicitly configurable physical influence distance capped to one World crossing. Constants are heuristic calibration. Seasonal contrast is provisional amplitude, not calendar weather. Accepted hydrology continues using the original uniform-runoff assumptions and unchanged solver.

Fifteen land families distinguish rainforest, tropical seasonal forest, temperate deciduous/coniferous forest, boreal forest, savanna, grassland, scrub, steppe, hot/cold desert, tundra, alpine, ice and wetland-associated landscapes. Numerical sea and accepted standing lakes remain authoritative masks. Confirmed creator wetlands can inform classification; potential wetland suggestions do not become certified permanent wetlands. Climate cannot remove an accepted lake or change a saved river.

Older generation prose and legacy forest/biome tendencies are not independently persisted climate. This pass does not reinterpret descriptions, rewrite original generation history or regenerate an old heightmap. Creators deliberately choose environmental assumptions over their accepted geography.
