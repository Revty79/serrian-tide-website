## Creator workflow

Previously, material selection lived in a technical inspector, selecting painting could change the appearance, and region creation was buried in World settings. Everyday controls now remain beside the map: Paint, Sculpt, Water, Places and Inspect, with separate **Choose Existing Region** and **Create New Region** actions. Advanced settings start closed. The active tool exposes its own controls rather than every layer at once.

Paint immediately exposes Ground Materials / Vegetation / Biomes, the selected material or category, physical Brush Size, Strength, and save feedback. Ground strokes autosave; environment coverage explicitly uses Save Changes. Short contextual descriptions distinguish ground cover from plants, broader biomes and elevation. Sculpt retains all seven tools and Original/Fine/Single Sample precision. Water offers river/lake entry actions and preview/save guidance. Optional Mapmaking ideas explain a creative sequence without requiring a tutorial. Refinement guidance distinguishes finer elevation samples from selecting a regional view or changing the appearance.

Selecting Paint preserves the selected and saved appearance in all six modes. An explicit temporary material preview can show painted materials in modes that do not display them; stopping it restores the exact selected appearance. Previewing does not dirty or save the appearance.

Create New Region presents Rectangle, Freehand and Point-by-Point directly. From a region it safely returns to the parent World. Pending appearance, geography, recovery or other drafts produce specific reasons and retain the work; a queued creation starts after the blocking work is resolved. Recovered outlines reactivate boundary controls. The canvas retains the original 500 px desktop and half-viewport phone/tablet gates, with contextual boundary controls and optional disclosures rather than a permanent sidebar.

## Accepted geometry and protection

Freehand records actual mouse/pen/touch coordinates. Point-by-Point supports undo, move, insert, remove and close. Both support preview/accept, an additional disconnected area, and navigation while unfinished. Two-finger navigation cancels only the accidental drawing gesture and retains the preceding outline. Physical simplification is explicit and retains the original if simplification would produce an invalid polygon. Browser draft recovery and stable pending requests survive reload and an actual Chrome profile restart.

One immutable footprint belongs to one regional identity: up to eight disjoint closed parts and 1,024 accepted vertices total, with 8,192 raw drawing points. Coordinates remain World metres, including wrapped seams and fractional positions on million-kilometre Worlds. Validation rejects self-intersections, overlaps/touching parts, nesting, degenerate geometry and ambiguous wrapped winding. **Holes are not supported.** Bounds are derived for indexing/viewing; they never replace the polygon as authorization.

Server checks use the accepted persisted footprint for material and vegetation brush radii, full paths/end caps, environmental polygons, water edits and connections, rotated artwork corners, geographic labels, Town placements, and wholly contained refinements. Concave bays and multipart gaps are forbidden even when they lie inside the bounding rectangle. Missing/corrupt footprint data and a partially missing geometry schema fail closed. Existing pre-0149 rectangle identities keep their original rectangular semantics and exact retry hashes. Geographic collections, numerical tiles and Town identities remain shared with the parent and overlapping regions; no parent grid or layer collection is copied into a region. Map appearance/composition remains map-local.
