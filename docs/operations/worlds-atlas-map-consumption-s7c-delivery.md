## Delivered boundary

The separate integrity script protects all other starting paths outside the new AGENTS section and verifies exact build inputs. ## Decisions without questions

- Require an explicit retained native version. Current-only maps must first be deliberately retained through existing authorized Atlas authoring; the producer cannot invent an approved immutable publication or silently capture current work.
- Make the consumer projection ordinary for all callers, including Owner and Administrator review. Existing Atlas privileged review remains untouched. The future approval/publication policy is separate.
- Preserve source family geometry and physical measurements, with bounded resources rather than a flattened picture or another storage model. Root and Region keep World metres; a Region is not translated twice.
- Exclude private/absent geometry and notes; resolve actual saved destinations through existing retained-source guards. Missing historical parents remain unknown instead of choosing a current Town/craft representation or inferring alignment.
- Keep uploaded asset references only when exact ordinary native pins and current scopes allow them; return no private bytes, paths or download URLs. Future private image delivery/redistribution requires its own approved consumer boundary.
- Separate requested/adopting timeline from the version's origin timeline; retain actual Canon source World/timeline/map/version identities without granting source access. Independently authored versions do not receive invented original-version provenance.
- Keep checksums as integrity/binding checks, not bearer access or publication signatures. Recheck permissions after slow reads and withhold resources on revocation or incompatible availability.
- Use new owned clones of the completed connected checkpoint fixture, retain failed/intermediate receipts and stop after verified publication. Do not modify shared targets or the prior completed stages.
