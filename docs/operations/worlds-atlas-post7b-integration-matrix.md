## Permissions applied to every link

Owner, limited Editor, limited Viewer, uninvited Player, explicit Administrator review, ordinary Canon Player and private-variation Player are separate cases. Link discovery never grants access. Related records, neighboring maps, exact source versions and private images each reauthorize the selected World and current permission. ## Deliberate limits

No physical position is invented for abstract or unplaced records. No current location fills unknown history. Existing accepted private Worlds retain their users' edits; only unfinished old preparations resume with the corrected selection under their original packet and assigned identities. Legacy political geometry retains its original space. Technical Chrome/touch-emulation evidence remains separate from owner artistic/usability acceptance and physical-device certification.
