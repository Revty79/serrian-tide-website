# Sustained automatic fire clarification

Authority: Brannan's response during Combat Pass 6, 8 September 2026.

Apply ammunition expenditure and the corresponding attack consequences at each completed Initiative point of firing. The declared duration includes the initial one-point trigger pull: three Initiative of firing costs three, with portions at 21, 20 and 19 from a start of 22. Separate preparation, cycling and recoil use their established authored costs.

An interruption after the portion at 20 retains the first two portions' spent ammunition and consequences, and cancels the remaining portion without its ammunition or damage. Freeze preserves the pending work until Resume.

Keep the single declaration Roll and simultaneous-declaration privacy. Do not add Rolls or apply the whole declaration's damage at every point. Allocate the existing firearm bullet-hit and damage calculation across the corresponding firing portions.
