# Combat spell resolution clarification

Authority: Brannan's combat testing instructions on 9 September 2026.

- A spell available in combat is a Skill owned by the character. Its own calculated percentage governs the casting Roll; the general magic framework percentage is not substituted.
- Use the shared successful Roll rules. Count the initial success, then another for each ten above the target: against 40, 50 is two successes and 60 is three.
- Multiply authored per-success effects by those successes. Keep static effects fixed. Preserve the existing spell modifier scope and casting cost calculations.
- Ordinary damaging spells use the standard hit-location rule and the target's authored anatomy. The original casting Roll supplies the location.
- Before mapped tabletop support, AoE casts calculate the damage/effect amount for the authored area and report it. The action finishes without waiting for mapped occupants or a G.O.D. area ruling. This temporary report does not apply HP or other effects to individual combatants.
- Area-only failed/critical casts also finish with a report identifying the Roll outcome. Critical outcomes are recorded without inventing extra area damage or effects.

The current implementation retains the original Roll, one Mana expenditure at casting start, and casting time before result completion. Direct spell effects use one result report and approval. Unsupported non-area effects retain their existing specific resolution boundary.
