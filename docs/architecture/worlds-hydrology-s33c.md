## Implemented interpretation and bounds

`terrain-water-natural.ts` ranks headwaters using contributing physical area, dominant upstream hierarchy, physical branch length, significance relative to the receiving channel and spacing between confluences. Sparse, balanced and fine recipes retain up to 24, 72 and 160 meaningful headwaters from at most 16,384 candidates. Their downstream paths follow the original unfilled numerical receivers exactly. Only selected incoming branches create natural-extraction junctions, avoiding the old fragmentation at subthreshold incoming cells. The original `calculateHydroFlow` body and the complete 3B adaptive mesh remain unchanged. Uniform artificial slopes may still produce straight channels.

Acceptance reuses compatible already accepted segments and their identities. Partial overlaps, ambiguous interior junctions and protected conflicts fail visibly rather than create competing routes. Existing Join tools and explicit replacement of unprotected derived networks remain available. The bounded overlap search permits one million segment probes. Natural suggestions never automatically rewrite old water; presentation-only suppression and degree-two path joining preserve every saved identity. Selected, manual, protected and explicitly width-authored channels remain visible. Display rounding is confined to unprotected derived routes, at most one fifth of their recorded minimum analysis spacing and one quarter of neighboring segment lengths; feature-anchored and authored routes stay exact.

The Canvas2D preparation cache retains up to four main layers and one transient watershed contour, with a conservative 32 MiB preparation estimate. Local-origin paths are reused across ordinary pan, zoom and appearance changes. Very long geometry can keep at most two additional camera-local anchors in 16 km buckets to avoid float precision loss at extreme zoom. The estimate includes these preparations but is not process RSS and excludes opaque browser/native overhead. All wrapped channel copies are painted before point symbols, so a later seam-crossing copy cannot cover a spring, waterfall or rapids symbol.

Special-feature review samples existing accepted routes inside the requested focus, including clipped long segments and wrapped geography. It is bounded to 65,536 samples, 128 samples per segment, 64 point proposals and 12 local wet-area patches. Spacing respects the coarsest actual source axis. A potential waterfall requires sampling at 50 m or finer, a short reach no longer than 150 m and significant drop/grade; coarser evidence is described as uncertain steep-reach/rapids evidence. These are candidate interpretations, never measured hydraulics. Wet-area review examines low-gradient geography near accepted rivers/lakes and produces bounded local patches of roughly 2,048 cells; it does not delineate an entire wetland ecosystem. Derived classifications remain `potential`. Unsupported or excessive contours are omitted with explicit review diagnostics. Creators define final extent, classification and intermittence.

Version 3 permits up to 512 special features. Wetland rings share the existing 32,768 surface-point budget with lakes/catchments. Existing analysis/graph budgets, worker cancellation and 120-second watchdog remain. Springs link only to deliberate headwater sources; linked movement preserves the channel identity. Parent changes retain related features and request review. Manual feature provenance includes every source overlapping its geographic extent, including refinements inside a boundary. Source invalidation is conservative. Finished features use existing recovery; unfinished boundary taps warn before leaving but must be finished to form a recoverable object.
