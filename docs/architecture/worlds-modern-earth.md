Generic immutable display metadata in the provenance selects permitted detail bands, priority and presentation style. It does not change protection. Existing imports remain compatible. Higher-priority readable representations for the same place avoid duplicate country outlines. Smaller countries missing from a coarse inventory use source-backed finer polygons at overview scale. Physical land and political interpretations have independent source namespaces and visibility controls; none create polities, control records or History events.

The spherical adapter validates polar caps in a pole-centered plane, normalizes only floating-point domain noise in disposable display copies and supports non-crossing boundary contacts without accepting crossing or retraced rings. Original coordinates stay unchanged. Complete source outlines at their selected scale avoid independent simplification gaps between adjacent political polygons. Equirectangular supports the poles; Mercator retains its explicit latitude limit.

Derived cache algorithm 3 draws administrative boundaries as lines rather than stroked closed projection polygons. It cancels overlapping opposite antimeridian cut intervals, including differently segmented source edges, and omits the single polar point. Genuine unpaired meridian edges, holes and unmatched source segments remain. This avoids invented borders at the wrapping edge or viewport clip. The 10m Fiji source has slightly unequal seam endpoints (about 0.0014 degrees on Taveuni); the display retains the tiny unmatched tails rather than altering authoritative coastlines. Original Geo A caches remain readable, and new cache rows do not replace original geometry or revisions.

The viewer requests automatic detail by viewport, progressively consumes bounded pages, cancels superseded contexts and retains credits. Each request returns at most 64 components and 500,000 path characters; oversized individual detail is rejected with retry and whole-surface recovery. Feature search and independent layer controls replace an unbounded button list. Opening a World with one eligible public World-surface view embeds its read-only geography, with a route to the workshop. Multiple or protected/Realm contexts require a deliberate choice.

Source inspection keeps original classifications and disputed accounts separate from an assertion of sovereignty. Ordinary and Administrator previews stay server-filtered and read-only. Geographic mutation routes honor both ordinary URL conventions and explicit review. The existing Atlas ordinary projection also excludes complete private geographic maps before serialization, including generic maps without a geographic place association. Source readers and loaded maps carry exact request-context keys through delayed, failed and retried requests.

Regional queries retain the existing spatial GiST index. For a validated full-surface extent, the query skips an unselective global spatial bitmap and ranks eligible readable source representations before bounded paging. This prevents repeated scans of the full spatial-part table per country. The original cartography renderer and its source/artwork persistence remain separate.

Natural Earth is generalized source cartography, not a survey or live political feed. Its default [administrative outlines](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-admin-0-countries/) follow the provider's de facto interpretation; [disputed areas](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-admin-0-breakaway-disputed-areas/) remain explicit independent overlays. No scale promises every islet.
