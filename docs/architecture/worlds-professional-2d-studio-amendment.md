The order above replaces the proposal's treatment of historical interior/dungeon layouts as a late independent afterthought. Existing generic/settlement historical appearances remain supported; no claim is made that current plan editors already support dated layout versions.

## Topology and connected geometry contract

Earth is one reference/surface, not the assumed shape of every World. Shared geometry, transforms and connected-map designs must identify the surface/topology separately from map/place identities and the current 2D view. Avoid irreversible Earth-only or rectangular-world identifiers. A rectangular canvas may remain a bounded presentation without defining the World's topology.

Before accepting a shared geometry or link contract, specify edge behavior: bounded/clamped edges, open boundaries, wrapped seams, adjacency/orientation changes, continuity of paths and selections, and explicit unsupported transitions. Record whether coordinates/units are local, planar or spherical; transforming a presentation must not silently reinterpret saved coordinates. Links must retain the destination place/map/version and the local entry/return context across scales and topology boundaries.

Plan interfaces for later spherical Worlds; flat square or triangular Worlds; hollow interiors; cylinders; rings and polyhedra; and creator-defined topologies. Do not implement those geometries, 3D rendering or terrain simulation in this phase. The first bounded increment below uses existing plane/sphere references and rejects unsupported transforms explicitly; its review must establish that future topology choices do not require replacing persistent identities.

## Visual and pack acceptance contract

Each regional, city, interior, dungeon and cave pass must produce multiple visually distinct original maps through normal website controls. Recolored copies, artwork boards and fixtures alone do not satisfy acceptance. Choose subjects and contexts that exercise meaningful variation in composition, packs, materials and scale; propose coverage based on use, rather than an arbitrary asset/map count.

Every pack brief must name its coverage and omissions, original sources and rights, exact references, intended scale and native detail, variation rules and budgets. Demonstrate the pack in actual maps and inspect full exports, native crops, reduced/phone views and intended print output. City evidence must include street/lot/roof structure; plans must include walls/openings/furniture and useful rooms/passages. Demonstrate real selection/editing, layers, failed save/retry/recovery and era switching on those maps. Review each pack's quality and coverage separately; one good regional island does not approve other scales or all packs.

## Editing, privacy, output and performance contracts

- **Identity:** Define duplicate/group/ungroup/mirror and selective-regeneration semantics before implementation. Geometry copies get distinct drawing/object IDs; reusing a persistent place is an explicit choice. Preserve originals, wall/opening attachments, relationships, pinned library revisions, exact historical pointers and deterministic retained seeds. Selective regeneration must state its boundary and preserve excluded authored objects; it cannot silently replace sources or places.
- **Layers and export:** Hide/lock/order are editing choices, not access grants. Enforce ordinary/protected/private knowledge on sources, selection, thumbnails, caches, connected navigation and every export. Unauthorized roles must never recover hidden protected data from a layer or downloadable file.
- **Erase and opacity:** State whether erasing removes a paint mark, edits a non-destructive mask or changes geometry. State object/layer opacity and compositing order, including transparent export behavior. Retain editable originals and deliberate undo/recovery; destructive source edits require an explicit separate action.
- **Lettering and print:** Specify label hierarchy/collision overrides, font licensing/embedding, legend and compass composition, grid type/origin/units, and calibrated physical scale with projection limits. Define page size, DPI, margins, tiling/overlap, line/text minimums, transparent/background options and permission-aware output. Inspect a native export and a measured print/PDF or equivalent print artifact; a browser screenshot alone is insufficient.
- **Performance:** Contract representative larger city and interior/dungeon scenes with declared object/point/attachment counts, pack bytes, viewport/zoom, device/browser, pixel/page output sizes and CPU/memory budgets. Measure selection/hit testing, initial and warm load, transforms, pan/touch, save/recovery and export, including cancellation, useful progress and bounded rejection. The UX-0A 100-drawing/1500-mark island budget remains its own gate and does not establish these capabilities. Agree exact larger workloads and thresholds before implementing each increment, then publish measured results and limits.
- **Cost and rights:** Preserve browser/CPU operation, lazy exact-reference assets and bounded derivatives. No required server GPU, paid AI/art service or unlicensed asset dependency. State real storage/backup/bandwidth costs and inspect rights for every import or pack.

For gate 5H, keep an unedited baseline and create two distinct dated/alternate versions of each accepted city/building/dungeon study. Edit one era, revisit the other and baseline, and prove their payloads remain unchanged. Pin and reopen a connected destination at the intended era/version; return to the source context; exercise ordinary/author/authorized-review access, unavailable-version handling, undo, failure/recovery and permission-filtered export. Dates, timeline identity, version identity and physical place identity remain separate. This is a proposed bounded gate, not authorization to extend schemas now.

## First bounded shared 2D increment proposed for owner consideration

**Candidate 2D-1: retained-object selection and transforms.** After UX-0A acceptance, prepare an implementation contract for a small shared selection/transform interaction integrated into the existing regional, settlement and plan editors. Limit the first approved contract to selecting existing editable objects, an explicit selection list, translation/rotation and reversible grouping of that selection. Grouping is an editor selection aid; it must not create or merge places. Define mirror/duplicate behavior and topology compatibility at the interface, but defer their implementation and selective regeneration to subsequent bounded contracts unless the owner explicitly expands this one.

Before coding, enumerate supported object types and dependencies (including attached openings), coordinate/reference assumptions, selection bounds, keyboard/touch/field parity, history transaction boundaries, locked/private object handling and pending-form/save conflict behavior. Keep the source/reference and persistent identity contracts above. If a durable group model or other schema change proves necessary, stop that part at a concrete proposal; do not silently add a migration.

Proposed acceptance: edit representative existing regional artwork, a street/building selection and a room/wall/opening selection using the same interaction; retain IDs, connections and exact asset references; show a single reversible operation with undo/redo, save/reopen, failure/retry/recovery and ordinary/review privacy checks. Supply desktop/touch evidence, unchanged-source checks, and measured selection/transform/load costs on the agreed larger city/plan fixtures. Explicitly reject unsupported topology/object combinations without modifying their sources. This increment does not promise materials, new artwork packs, print systems, era schemas, full professional-map acceptance or numerical terrain.

**Owner decision requested later:** approve or revise this amended sequence and select the first bounded contract. Planning review or technical acceptance alone does not authorize implementation or lock the roadmap.


## Owner feedback added during N0 review (planning only)

The owner-authorized N0 on PR #7 now includes generic-map saved-item selection, contextual transforms, an Items list and visible canvas controls over existing contracts. Candidate 2D-1 above therefore concerns **shared interaction across regional, settlement and plan editors**, supported multi-object selection and reversible grouping; it must not duplicate N0 or imply these broader capabilities have already shipped.

N1 remains proposal/contract work: curated editable regional/city/era/interior/dungeon starts; asset search/favorites and original coherent multi-era packs; previewable smart forest/road/block/room/wall authoring; snap/guides and handles; reversible material masks/eraser; duplicate/group and contextual defaults; opt-in label-overlap, river-outlet and waterfront cues. Every generated result must keep editable sources, stable places and historical pins, reversible transactions and explicit supported limits. Separate bounded contracts must define their order, assets, rights, costs and larger-scene budgets before implementation.

**Painted marks:** N0 retains one deterministic terrain stroke and its exact asset set. Its individual generated trees are not independently persistent objects. There is no UI-only operation in the current contract that can retain a mark identity and independently transform or suppress that mark across a changed stroke seed/density/asset set. Do not flatten a stroke or silently rewrite its seed to simulate this.

Two future mechanisms need an owner decision: (a) preview an explicit conversion of chosen deterministic instances into version-pinned independent decorative symbols, retain the original stroke and record reversible exclusions; (b) keep the stroke and add bounded stroke-local exclusions/transform overrides with an explicit stable mark-key policy. Both require a model/validation/history contract (and potentially a reviewed additive migration), not merely a selection UI. Specify what happens after changing seed, spacing, density, radius, points or asset revisions; how exclusions are rebased or reported unresolved; maximum overrides and selection/export costs; exact undo/recovery and era payload behavior; rights/privacy filtering and no implicit creation/deletion of persistent places. Prototype and benchmark only after a separately approved bounded contract. No per-mark model or migration is implemented by this checkpoint.
