## Implementation details and limits

`climate-v1` uses at most 256 by 256 global cells and 128 by 128 local cells. Up to eight accepted fields share a 262,144-cell budget. Global analysis samples the accepted base; local downscaling requires matching accepted global assumptions and uses bounded source/refinement loading. Each cell stores calibrated mean temperature in tenths C, relative moisture, biome, vegetation, density and provisional seasonal contrast. The 16-step physical upwind horizon is capped to one World crossing. Configurable influence distance, altitude cooling and heuristic barrier coefficients are deliberate modeling assumptions, not atmospheric measurements. Coarse context cannot resolve every tiny island or mountain barrier.

The initial families are rainforest, tropical seasonal forest, temperate deciduous/coniferous forest, boreal forest, savanna, grassland, scrub, steppe, hot/cold desert, tundra, alpine, ice and wetland-associated landscape; water remains a separate numerical/hydrological mask. Vegetation adds independently editable type, density, illustrative scale and clustering. Mean temperatures and moisture are inspected only where accepted climate exists; manual coverage alone makes no climate claim.

Automatic artwork transitions use a fixed two-analysis-cell separable filter, independent of camera and rendering tiles. The original accepted classifications remain exact and visible in diagnostic overlays. Local fields blend over two cells at their boundaries. Manual biome regions precede independent vegetation regions; each layer has its own erase-to-automatic operation. Global/selected regeneration preserves all overrides by default. Explicit replacement can remove only unprotected regions wholly inside the reviewed frame. Protected identities and geometry always remain.

Artwork is original procedural Canvas2D code, with no new external imagery or runtime service. Stable jittered crowns, irregular silhouettes, overlapping canopy, foliage variation, ground texture and physical-frequency filtering distinguish close cover from regional generalization. These are illustrations of coverage, not surveyed trees or species. The original terrain sampler supplies relief; the original terrain, material, cartographic and water painters are retained. A geographic tile covering roughly 128 device pixels prepares a 64-square environmental image, then recomputes at finer physical LODs as required; it does not enlarge a single World texture through subsequent zooms. A one-pixel halo and shared geographic sampling preserve joins.

Each editor owns a cancellable CPU worker for preparation; ordinary pan/zoom reuses whole physical tiles. A time-sliced Canvas2D main-thread fallback remains available. Artwork bitmaps are limited to 32 MiB. Diagnostics separately estimate source/decoded fields and retained transition arrays; transient filter arrays, structured-clone copies, JavaScript, native canvas and process RSS are additional. Worst-case overlapping manual geometry can still cost more than sparse ordinary authoring. Up to 512 regions / 32,768 points share indexed geographic bins and pre-unwrapped polygon paths. Analysis has a 120-second watchdog and artwork jobs a 45-second worker watchdog.

Environment uses explicit independent saves, immutable append-only history, exact original-request retry, conflict retention/export and browser recovery. Finished brush/polygon edits recover; unfinished boundary taps must be finished first and warn on leaving. Up to 12 undo states share a point budget. Source changes retain accepted fields and protected authoring, show a review warning, and require deliberate global review before new local downscaling. Terrain autosaving and water saves do not discard an environmental draft or clear its navigation guard.
