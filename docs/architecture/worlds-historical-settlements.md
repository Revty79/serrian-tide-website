# Historical settlements: Phase 6B-B design

Separately authorized Phase 6B-C adds [historical political overlays](worlds-historical-territories.md) to these same saved settlement appearances. It preserves place conditions, stable settlement/geographic identities, physical layouts and the original renderer. Political source periods and accounts remain independent of settlement existence and actual History event dates. Phase 6B-D coordinated historical connections and historical interior/dungeon editing remain separate scope.

Approved starting point: `9267fc465e717d9503f4e5546e87b6243a69741b`. Extend the existing appearance identity, immutable version, timeline head, source pin and optimistic revision model. No second map identity, editor or renderer is introduced.

Settlement versions capture the real Settlement Editor's shapes, semantic entity categories, local classifications and district memberships, layer state, cartographic background, local names/descriptions and explicit place conditions. The existing editor and SettlementScene read that captured source. Archived drawings remain source records; archiving is independent of historical destruction. Conditions are present, absent, not yet established, abandoned, ruined, destroyed, rebuilt and unknown. Abandoned, ruined and rebuilt remain visible; absent, not yet established, destroyed and unknown do not draw a present place. The author can still select their retained source in the workshop. A condition on the settlement itself controls its entire representation.

Shared World geography and settlement entities remain authoritative identities. Historical edits never change their names, parents, kinds, ownership or memberships. Authors explicitly create a new or replacement place in the shared Settlement Editor before reusing it historically; a later appearance can draw another representation of that saved place. Copying a representation does not invent identity continuity. Earlier appearances retain omitted/archived geometry and exact original source accounts. Local labels, classifications, memberships, positions, boundaries and artwork are versioned independently.

One additive migration after 0124 extends bounded payload validation and representation reservations to settlement geometry. Deferred checks bind every shape to its saved entity, stable category, map and settlement and every condition to a retained geography reference. Shared writers respect historical representation reservations. Existing generic source bytes and migrations remain unchanged. The 4,000-object, 64,000-point and 12 MB request bounds still apply. Settlement source allows 4,129 geography entries, accounting for its captured subject alongside the previously supported 4,128 place entries.

Reuse inclusive periods, unknown gaps, exact direct-parent retention and independent branch interpretations. Saving a chart creates no History event. Authors may cite an existing event's exact identity/version in the historical source account; authoritative History and calendar records are untouched. Rich source comparisons and historical connection changes remain later passes.

Ordinary choices are filtered by the existing source/geography protection function before serialization. A selected settlement projection removes unavailable places and their descendants, shapes, memberships, associated maps and inverse connections on the server; current baseline settlement details must never fill missing historical entities. Unadopted parent choices remain absent. Author/review readers preserve full immutable source; Administrator and ordinary contexts remain read-only. The approved Explorer request-context guard continues to cover both metadata and rendered charts.

Validate fresh and populated-6B-A upgrades, reapplication, database invariants, source preservation, branch/privacy behavior, recovery and conflicts in disposable databases. Use authenticated production-server desktop and native-touch checks for real settlement geometry, historical navigation and editor reuse. Stop at a clean local commit for independent technical review and owner visual acceptance, without push, deployment or shared database/service changes.

## Delivered behavior and recovery

Open a saved settlement from Atlas, choose its shared baseline or a saved appearance, or enter a canonical World year. Create an appearance from the displayed source and save its inclusive period before editing. The Settlement Editor saves layout and place conditions into that selected timeline's immutable source. Terrain and artwork opens the original Map Maker inside the same historical workspace and returns to the settlement surface. Explore and connected-map return preserve the requested year, including unknown destination coverage. Reload opens the same settlement and year through `editMap` and `mapYear`.

Names, descriptions, classifications and district memberships are local in a historical appearance. New shapes may reuse places registered after the captured source; their shared read-only archival flag is not copied into the editable draft, and uncaptured district memberships are not silently adopted. Previously absent geometry stays retained and can be selected through the workshop's object list. Duplicating a historical representation keeps its chosen place identity; creating a replacement identity remains an explicit shared-settlement operation. Conditions do not change protection, global ancestry or chronology.

Ordinary projections withhold source account text and remove unavailable local names, descendants, memberships, associated maps and inverse connections. Full snapshot geography is never copied into the serialized map header. Author and explicit Administrator readers retain exact source accounts and earlier revisions; Administrator review cannot author. Explorer accepts chart and appearance metadata together only for the exact World, map, timeline, year and knowledge/review context, including loading, failure and retry.

Apply `0125_worlds_historical_settlements.sql` after 0124 and every predecessor in journal order. It extends the existing payload validator and object constraint, replaces the deferred source and shared-object guards, and protects the semantic category and subject of maps and settlement entities with retained history. No new table, column, backfill, external service or configuration is required. The original generic payload validator remains available unchanged; the version check is explicitly rebound to the extended validator. Reapplication preserves retained sources.

Use a verified backup, paused writers, the committed migrator and a staged production build after separate authorization. Reverting code does not reverse migration 0125 or its ledger entry. Older 6B-A code cannot expose historical settlement authoring; preserve all appearance tables and source data and keep incompatible Atlas writers paused during fallback. Prefer a reviewed forward fix. Controlled backup recovery requires preserving subsequent changes and reconciling application and schema versions.

Appearance saves that fail keep the editor draft and offer download or comparison. If a write succeeds but readback fails, recovery reads the acknowledged saved appearance without issuing another creation. Concurrent editors compare against the selected timeline head; one simultaneous save succeeds and the other receives a conflict. Archive and restore append sources without deleting geometry or identity.
