## Implemented interpretation and bounds

The original 3A numerical flow body is unchanged. Priority flood adds a separate filled-level/escape-parent diagnostic; it never changes accepted elevations or the unfilled single-receiver graph. A suggestion starts below its sampled spill, remains closed, and explicitly says that persistent water is provisional. Spill review can propose an outlet along the escape route. It refuses to claim a route through another unreviewed impoundment; creators can connect accepted lakes deliberately. Lake connections participate in graph cycle checks alongside river segments.

The shoreline uses bounded local sampling and marching squares, including island holes. Analysis-cell extents bound contour coverage across unequal-resolution interfaces. Watershed area sums actual contributing land rectangles; its outline is an approximate display of that sampled membership. Wrapped watersheds covering the entire circumference use a canonical meridian cut without drawing the artificial cut as a geographic boundary. A lake covering the entire circumference is explicitly refused rather than invented as an ordinary polygon.

Local requests keep distant refinements at accepted coarse overview levels, preserving connected downstream context while allocating finer samples near the requested geography. This improves local analysis resolution; it does not certify distant small channels at that resolution. Retained analysis pins identify the actual source revisions, hashes, overview levels and spacings. Manual lakes can retain accepted source-head pins as authoring context; those pins do not claim sampled bathymetry. Source-head changes conservatively flag affected objects until deliberate review.

Accepting a lake reconciles existing unprotected derived courses beneath its surface, retaining upstream inlet connections. Protected and authored courses remain intact and cause an explicit review flag when necessary. A moved lake port retains its identity and marks the lake protected and needing review; alignment is a separate deliberate operation. Deleting a lake preserves rivers as explicit detached endpoints. Closing an outlet also retains its downstream course for review. Every operation remains one undoable, revisioned water document.

The existing 524,288 planned-cell and 655,360 mesh-cell limits remain. Water geometry retains 8,192 nodes, 4,096 channels, 65,536 channel points, 512 points per channel and 32 analyses. The extension allows 256 lakes, 64 retained catchments and 32,768 combined surface points, with 8,192 points per ring. Automatic lake suggestions are limited to 64; local contour grids are bounded at 512 by 512. Long escape routes or requests exceeding geometry limits are refused explicitly without discarding accepted authoring. The worker remains cancellable with the existing 120-second watchdog. Typed-array accounting includes contour workspaces, but excludes JavaScript/browser overhead.

Uniform runoff means relative contributing area, not rainfall or discharge. Automatic river weight is symbolic and separate from a creator's physical width override. Single-receiver directional bias, provisional water permanence, coarse distant context and bounded shoreline accuracy remain explicit limitations. Completed draft operations recover through the original separate water recovery mechanism; an unfinished tap-by-tap shoreline sketch warns before leaving but must be finished to become a recoverable water object.
