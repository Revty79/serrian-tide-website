Manual placements use canonical physical World metres, stable IDs, asset/pack versions, geographic width, rotation and opacity. They survive refinements, pack changes and automatic regeneration. Automatic decoration uses only accepted numerical, environmental and water data. Stable canonical geographic lattice cells and deterministic seed/cluster selection anchor bounded objects across seams. Water masks reject dry-land motifs in oceans/standing lakes; confirmed wetlands, biome/vegetation coverage, real relief and climate choose suitable motifs. Density and visibility do not change source data. The planner culls invisible cells, limits candidates and rendered placements, suppresses subpixel symbols, and reuses prepared symbol bitmaps. Whole-world views emphasize restrained masses; regional and close scales reveal appropriate detail. Geographic truth and visual scale remain distinct.

Existing maps start with automatic artwork disabled and no manual placements. Enabling packs replaces the older environmental symbol glyph presentation while retaining environmental ground and accepted source data. Turning packs off restores that presentation. Automatic objects are regenerated deterministically from the current accepted geography; artwork history restores artwork settings and placements, not earlier terrain, water or climate. Manual material overrides suppress automatic decoration in their affected areas. Volcanoes and reefs remain deliberate manual choices because the current accepted classifiers do not establish those geological facts.

It prepares no samplers for an empty/disabled layer and no environmental sampler for manual-only artwork. At most 2,400 automatic candidates and 450 selected symbols are considered per plan, manual records are capped at 1,024, and invisible/wrapped copies are culled. Automatic detail uses deterministic scale bands; manual anchors and physical dimensions remain exact. The 320-screen-pixel display cap is shared by drawing and selection.
