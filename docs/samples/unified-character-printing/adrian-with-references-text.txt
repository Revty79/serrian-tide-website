◆Recorded skills & powers
Spellcraft → Water
Channeling
Points 3 · Rank 19 · Target 26%+
Points 10 · Rank 16 · Target 29%+
Water magic embodies flow, pressure, and adaptability. It
may cleanse, crush, or conceal. Practitioners learn to
harness water’s many forms—vapor, ice, and tide alike.
Channeling grants your character the ability to act as a
conduit for supernatural energy. Whether divine, arcane,
or elemental, this skill determines how much mana or
power can be safely drawn into the body. The more
practiced the channel, the more intense and sustained the
force they can wield. Required to effectively channel magic
from any sphere.
Spellcraft → Time & Space
Points 3 · Rank 19 · Target 26%+
Time & Space magic manipulates flow, position, and
perception. It may accelerate or delay actions, fold
distances, or create pockets of stillness. This is a delicate
sphere—one misstep can echo far beyond the present.
Meditation
Points 10 · Rank 16 · Target 29%+
Meditation cultivates internal stillness and spiritual clarity.
Through this practice, your character recovers mana,
centers their will, and fortifies their connection to unseen
energies. Meditation is required to restore mana after
magical exertion.
Spellcraft → Divination
Points 3 · Rank 19 · Target 26%+
Divination reveals what is hidden—past, future, truth, or
lie. It sharpens intuition, enhances perception, and peels
back the veil. This magic doesn’t guarantee answers—it
opens the door to seeing them.
Spellcraft
Points 10 · Rank 16 · Target 29%+
Spellcraft is the intellectual mastery of magical form and
function. With it, characters access magical spheres and
manipulate spell structures with intent and precision. This
skill grants access to magical spheres for casting spells.
