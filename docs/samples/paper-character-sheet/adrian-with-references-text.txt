Eidetic Memory · passive · Available / INT 40+
You may automatically recall ordinary information that your character personally and clearly observed, read, heard, or
learned. No roll is required merely to remember that information. An INT-based roll may still be required when the
information was only partially perceived, deliberately concealed, experienced while severely distracted, or when extremely
precise details are important. Eidetic Memory does not provide knowledge you never acquired, reveal information you
failed to perceive, or grant understanding of something simply because you remember it.

Eidetic Memory
passive · Available
Your memory retains information with exceptional clarity. Things you deliberately observe, read, hear, or study can be recalled
with unusual accuracy long after most people would have forgotten the details.
You may automatically recall ordinary information that your character personally and clearly observed, read, heard, or
learned. No roll is required merely to remember that information.
An INT-based roll may still be required when the information was only partially perceived, deliberately concealed,
experienced while severely distracted, or when extremely precise details are important.
Eidetic Memory does not provide knowledge you never acquired, reveal information you failed to perceive, or grant
understanding of something simply because you remember it.
Requirements: INT 40+
